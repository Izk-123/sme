Definitely. If we're building this as a **Malawi-first, mobile-first Business OS for informal and small businesses**, I would think beyond traditional ERP features.

The question for every feature should be:

> **Does this reduce cost, increase revenue, reduce errors, save time, or improve decisions?**

## 🧩 Feature map

I would organize the platform roughly like this:

```text
                    SME BUSINESS OS
```

### Money Out

Then:

# 13. 📴 Offline Mode

This could become a major differentiator.

Imagine a shop loses internet.

They can still:

```text
Sell
↓
Record expense
↓
Receive stock
↓
View products
```

Data is stored locally.

When internet returns:

```text
Local PWA
     ↓
Sync
     ↓
Django
```

The user doesn't have to think about connectivity.

**Don't build a feature because "ERPs have it."**

Build it because a Malawian business owner can say:

> **"This saves me time."**

> **"This stops my employees from making mistakes."**

> **"This shows me where my money is going."**

> **"This helps me sell more."**

> **"This tells me what I should do next."**

That's what will make this a genuinely useful **SME Business OS**, rather than just another ERP clone.
