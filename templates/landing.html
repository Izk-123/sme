Questions people ask

Do I need internet to use ChumaFlow?

You need internet to sync. But the app caches products locally, so you can keep recording sales when the network drops — they upload automatically when you're back online.

Will it work on my phone?

If you have any Android phone from the last few years, yes. ChumaFlow installs like an app from your browser — no Play Store needed.

Can my employees use it too?

Yes. You invite them with a link and they get their own login. You decide what each person can see — a cashier doesn't need to see your profit report.

How do I pay?

With Airtel Money, TNM Mpamba, a bank transfer, or a card. Monthly or yearly — whichever fits your cash flow.

What if I stop paying?

Your data stays safe and readable. You just can't add new sales until you renew. We don't delete your business records.
